=== TEACHING EXAMPLES ===

Example 1:
User: [Context: CHEMISTRY] What is the hybridization of carbon in CH4?
Assistant: 
### 🧠 Core Concept
In methane (CH4), carbon forms four single covalent bonds with four hydrogen atoms, requiring four identical hybrid orbitals to minimize electron repulsion.

### ⚙️ Step-by-Step Breakdown
1. The ground state electronic configuration of Carbon (Z=6) is $1s^2 2s^2 2p^2$.
2. To form four bonds, one $2s$ electron gets excited to the empty $2p_z$ orbital, giving $1s^2 2s^1 2p^3$.
3. The one $2s$ and three $2p$ orbitals mix (hybridize) to form four equivalent $sp^3$ hybrid orbitals.
4. These orbitals arrange tetrahedrally at an angle of 109.5° to minimize repulsion.

### ⚠️ Common JEE Trap
Do not assume that the $s$ and $p$ orbitals overlap with hydrogen independently. If they did, bond lengths and angles would differ. The orbitals fully hybridize into structurally identical $sp^3$ orbitals first!

Example 2:
User: [Context: PHYSICS] Provide the formula for time of flight in projectile motion.
Assistant:
### 🧠 Core Concept
The time of flight is the total duration a projectile remains in the air before hitting the same horizontal plane it was launched from.

### ⚙️ Step-by-Step Breakdown
1. Consider the vertical motion equation: $y = u_y t + \frac{1}{2} a_y t^2$
2. For a full flight returning to the ground, displacement $y = 0$.
3. The initial vertical velocity is $u_y = u \sin(\theta)$ and acceleration is $a_y = -g$.
4. Setting the equation to zero: $0 = (u \sin(\theta))t - \frac{1}{2}gt^2$
5. Solving for $t$ (ignoring $t=0$):
   $t = \frac{2u \sin(\theta)}{g}$

### ⚠️ Common JEE Trap
Remember this formula is ONLY valid if the launch and landing points are on the exact same horizontal level. If launching off a cliff, you must use the full quadratic equation instead!
